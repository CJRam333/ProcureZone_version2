# User Story: Supervisor Role

## Role Overview

**Role ID**: 4  
**Database Field**: `supervisor` (session variable: `#session.Supervisor`)  
**Hierarchy Position**: Mid-level oversight, manages a team/section within a department  
**Primary Responsibility**: Monitor team activities, view team indents, generate section reports, support team members

### Permissions Matrix (From Database)
```
supervisor_id: 4
role_name: Supervisor
role_view: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_add: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_edit: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_delete: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_status: 1

Workflow-Specific Permissions:
- team_indents_view: TRUE (can view team members' indents)
- team_reports: TRUE (can generate section/team reports)
- material_search: TRUE (can search material catalog)
- master_data_view: TRUE (can view master data read-only)
- indent_create: TRUE (inherits User role capabilities)
- indent_view_own: TRUE (inherits User role capabilities)
```

### Key Characteristics
- **Team Oversight**: Can view all indents created by team members
- **Monitoring Role**: Tracks team indent status and material requests
- **No Approval Rights**: Cannot approve indents (that's Department Head's job)
- **Reporting Access**: Can generate reports for their section/team
- **Hybrid Role**: Has User role capabilities PLUS team monitoring
- **Section Scoped**: Limited to their assigned section(s) within department

## Typical Daily Workflows

### Morning Routine (8:30 AM - 9:00 AM)
1. **Login** → Supervisor Dashboard shows:
   - ❌ My team's pending indents count
   - ❌ Overdue approvals count (pending > 3 days)
   - ❌ My own draft/pending indents
   - ❌ Team activity summary (yesterday)

2. **Check Team Status**:
   - ❌ Review team indents submitted yesterday
   - ❌ Check for overdue approvals (pending > 3 days)
   - ❌ Identify any rejected indents (may need to discuss with team member)

3. **Monitor Pending Approvals**:
   - ❌ View indents pending with Department Head
   - ❌ Note any urgent indents that need follow-up
   - ❌ Prepare to escalate if needed (manual contact)

### During the Day (On-Demand)
1. **Team Member Support**:
   - ❌ Team member asks: "Has my indent been approved?"
   - ❌ Supervisor checks team indents view → finds indent
   - ❌ Provides status update (pending with DeptHead since X days)
   - ❌ Offers to escalate if urgent

2. **Material Request Monitoring**:
   - ❌ Check what materials team is requesting
   - ❌ Identify if similar materials already requested recently
   - ❌ Discuss with team member to consolidate requests if possible

3. **Create Own Indents** (as needed):
   - ❌ Create indent for own material needs
   - ❌ Same process as User role
   - ❌ Separate from team monitoring activities

### Weekly Tasks (Usually Friday afternoon)
1. **Generate Weekly Report**:
   - ❌ Generate section indent summary (last 7 days)
   - ❌ Review team productivity (indents per employee)
   - ❌ Check material usage trends
   - ❌ Export to Excel and email to Department Head

2. **Team Performance Review**:
   - ❌ Check team member-wise indent count
   - ❌ Check approval success rate per member
   - ❌ Identify training needs (e.g., high rejection rate)
   - ❌ Plan 1-on-1 discussions if needed

3. **Material Usage Analysis**:
   - ❌ Review top 10 materials requested
   - ❌ Identify materials with increasing demand
   - ❌ Discuss with team about bulk ordering opportunities
   - ❌ Report findings to Department Head

## Security Considerations

### What Supervisor CAN Do:
- ✅ View all indents created by team members (own section only)
- ✅ View team member list and basic info
- ✅ View team member indent history and statistics
- ✅ Generate reports for own section
- ✅ Track pending approvals for team
- ✅ View team-wide material usage trends
- ✅ Create own indents (inherits User role)
- ✅ View own indents (inherits User role)

### What Supervisor CANNOT Do:
- ❌ View indents from other sections/teams (out of scope)
- ❌ Edit team members' indents
- ❌ Approve or reject any indents (no approval authority)
- ❌ Delete team members' indents
- ❌ View employees from other teams
- ❌ Modify master data
- ❌ Access admin functions
- ❌ Generate organization-wide reports (only section reports)

## Database Considerations

### Required Mappings (May Not Exist Yet)

1. **Employee-Supervisor Mapping**
   - Table: `emp_supervisor` (or column in `employee` table: `supervisor_id`)
   - Purpose: Define who supervises whom
   - Query: Get all employees where `supervisor_id = current_user_emp_id`

2. **Section-Supervisor Mapping**
   - Table: `section_supervisor` (or column in `section` table: `supervisor_id`)
   - Purpose: Define which supervisor oversees which section
   - Query: Get all sections where `supervisor_id = current_user_emp_id`

3. **Team Definition**
   - **Option A**: Section-based (supervisor oversees entire section)
   - **Option B**: Direct employee assignment (supervisor → employees mapping)
   - **Legacy likely uses**: Section-based (section has supervisor, all section employees are the team)

**Recommendation**: If mapping tables don't exist, prioritize creating them in Phase 1.
