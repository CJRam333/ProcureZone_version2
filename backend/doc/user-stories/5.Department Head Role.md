# User Story: Department Head Role

## Role Overview

**Role ID**: 5  
**Database Field**: `dept_head` (session variable: `#session.DeptHead`)  
**Hierarchy Position**: Department-level authority, reports to senior management  
**Primary Responsibility**: Approve/reject department indents, manage department budget, oversee department operations

### Permissions Matrix (From Database)
```
dept_head_id: 5
role_name: DepartmentHead
role_view: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_add: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_edit: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_delete: FALSE (uses workflow-specific permissions)
role_status: 1

Workflow-Specific Permissions:
- indent_approve: TRUE (can approve/reject department indents)
- indent_view_all_dept: TRUE (can view all department indents)
- budget_view: TRUE (can view department budget)
- team_reports: TRUE (can generate department-wide reports)
- material_search: TRUE (can search material catalog)
- master_data_view: TRUE (can view master data read-only)
- indent_create: TRUE (inherits User role capabilities)
- indent_view_own: TRUE (inherits User role capabilities)
```

### Key Characteristics
- **Approval Authority**: Primary approver for department indents
- **Budget Oversight**: Monitors department material budget and spending
- **Department-Wide View**: Can see all indents across department (all sections)
- **Delegation**: Can delegate approval authority to other DeptHeads temporarily
- **Workflow Critical**: Bottleneck in indent workflow - indents wait for DeptHead approval
- **Hybrid Role**: Can create own indents + approve others' indents + monitor department

## User Stories

### 1. Indent Approval Workflow

#### 1.1 View Pending Approvals
**User Story**: "As a Department Head, I can view all indents pending my approval so that I can review and approve material requests for my department."

**Acceptance Criteria**:

- ❌ Can see indent creator name, submission date, total value (if applicable)
- ❌ Can see priority/urgency indicator (if set by creator)
- ❌ Can sort by submission date (oldest first)
- ❌ Can filter by section within department
- ❌ Can filter by material category
- ❌ Can see indent details (materials, quantities, justifications)
- ❌ Can see approval workflow history (if indent was forwarded from Supervisor)
- ❌ **Cannot** see indents from other departments (out of scope)

**API Endpoints Required**:

## Typical Daily Workflows

### Morning Routine (8:00 AM - 9:00 AM)
1. **Login** → DeptHead Dashboard shows:
   - ❌ Pending approvals count (high priority!)
   - ❌ Overdue approvals (pending > 3 days)
   - ❌ Budget status (consumed vs available)
   - ❌ Yesterday's department activity summary

2. **Review Pending Approvals**:
   - ❌ Open "Pending Approvals" page
   - ❌ Sort by oldest first (fairness)
   - ❌ Identify urgent/high-priority indents
   - ❌ Plan approval order

3. **Check Overdue Approvals**:
   - ❌ View overdue indents (pending > 3 days)
   - ❌ Prioritize these for immediate review
   - ❌ Apologize to requesters if delayed (email)

### Approval Processing (9:00 AM - 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM)
**Target**: Approve 10-20 indents per session

1. **For Each Indent**:
   - ❌ Open indent details
   - ❌ Review materials, quantities, justifications
   - ❌ Check if budget allows (especially high-value indents)
   - ❌ Verify if request is reasonable
   - ❌ Decision: Approve / Reject / Request More Info

2. **Approve**:
   - ❌ Add approval comments (optional but recommended)
   - ❌ Click "Approve All" or "Approve Selected"
   - ❌ Confirm action
   - ❌ Move to next indent

3. **Reject**:
   - ❌ Add rejection reason (mandatory, detailed)
   - ❌ Click "Reject All" or "Reject Selected"
   - ❌ Confirm action
   - ❌ Consider calling employee to explain (if major rejection)

4. **Request More Info**:
   - ❌ Specify what information is needed (clear instructions)
   - ❌ Click "Request More Information"
   - ❌ Confirm action
   - ❌ Move to next indent

### Mid-Day Tasks (11:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
1. **Monitor Department Activity**:
   - ❌ Check department indent list (all statuses)
   - ❌ Review material usage patterns
   - ❌ Check if any unusual requests

2. **Budget Review** (weekly, usually Monday or Friday):
   - ❌ View department budget status
   - ❌ Check spending trends
   - ❌ Identify if nearing budget limit
   - ❌ Plan cost-control measures if needed

### Afternoon Tasks (Variable)
1. **Handle Info Requests** (if indents came back with info):
   - ❌ Review updated indents
   - ❌ Re-evaluate with new information
   - ❌ Approve or reject

2. **Delegation Management** (before vacation):
   - ❌ Create delegation to another DeptHead
   - ❌ Specify delegation period
   - ❌ Notify delegate
   - ❌ Brief delegate on pending items

3. **Create Own Indents** (as needed):
   - ❌ Create indent for own needs
   - ❌ Same process as User role
   - ❌ Submit (may go to senior management or procurement)

### End-of-Day Review (5:00 PM - 5:30 PM)
1. **Check Approval Progress**:
   - ❌ How many approvals completed today
   - ❌ How many still pending
   - ❌ Any overdue approvals remaining

2. **Plan for Tomorrow**:
   - ❌ Note high-priority indents for tomorrow
   - ❌ Note if any urgent follow-ups needed

### Weekly Tasks (Friday afternoon)
1. **Generate Weekly Report**:
   - ❌ Department indent summary (last 7 days)
   - ❌ Approval performance (my metrics)
   - ❌ Material usage trends
   - ❌ Budget status
   - ❌ Export and email to senior management

2. **Review Approval Metrics**:
   - ❌ Check average approval time
   - ❌ Check approval rate
   - ❌ Identify if performance needs improvement
