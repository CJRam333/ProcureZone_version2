
# User Story: PlantManager, FloorIncharge, and DataEntryOperator Roles

This document combines three related plant operation roles for efficiency.

---

# 1. PlantManager Role

## Role Overview

**Role ID**: 7  
**Database Field**: `plant_manager` (session variable: `#session.PlantManager`)  
**Hierarchy Position**: Plant-level authority, manages plant operations  
**Primary Responsibility**: Oversee plant operations, approve plant indents, monitor material flow, manage plant employees

### Key Characteristics
- Plant-level oversight (not department-specific)
- Approves plant-specific indents
- Monitors material receipts and issues at plant
- Views plant inventory and floor operations
- Cross-department access within plant

### User Stories Summary

#### 1.1 Plant Indent Management
- ❌ View all plant indents (all departments at this plant)
- ❌ Approve plant-specific indents (similar to DeptHead but plant-scoped)
- ❌ Monitor indent status for plant

#### 1.2 Plant Operations Monitoring
- ❌ View plant dashboard (receipts, issues, inventory today)
- ❌ Monitor floor-wise operations
- ❌ View plant employee activities
- ❌ Track plant-wise material consumption

#### 1.3 Plant Reports
- ❌ Generate plant-level reports (receipts, issues, inventory)
- ❌ Material flow analysis
- ❌ Plant productivity metrics

**API Endpoints Required**: ~18
- Plant indent viewing: 4 endpoints
- Plant approval: 3 endpoints
- Plant monitoring: 5 endpoints
- Plant reports: 4 endpoints
- Plant inventory: 2 endpoints

**Legacy Features**: `/PlantIndentIsuueNote/plant_indent_request.jsp`, `/PlantIndentIsuueNote/plant_Inventory_mgr_list.jsp`  
**Current Status**: ❌ 0% - No plant operations module

# 2. FloorIncharge Role

## Role Overview

**Role ID**: 8  
**Database Field**: `floor_incharge` (session variable: `#session.FloorIncharge`)  
**Hierarchy Position**: Plant floor supervisor, manages material receipt at floor level  
**Primary Responsibility**: Receive materials at plant floor, create receipt notes, coordinate with GRNIncharge, manage floor inventory

### Key Characteristics
- **Critical in GRN workflow**: First to receive materials physically
- Creates receipt notes for materials delivered by vendors
- Coordinates with GRNIncharge for formal GRN creation
- Manages floor-level temporary storage
- Quality check before formal GRN (basic inspection)

### User Stories Summary

#### 2.1 Material Receipt at Floor
- ❌ View pending POs for my plant (expected deliveries)
- ❌ Record material receipt (when vendor delivers)
- ❌ Create receipt note with quantities received
- ❌ Add receipt remarks (condition, packaging, etc.)
- ❌ Upload receipt photos (optional)
- ❌ Basic quality check (visible defects)

#### 2.2 Receipt Note Management
- ❌ View my receipt notes (all statuses)
- ❌ Edit draft receipt notes
- ❌ Submit receipt note to GRNIncharge
- ❌ Track receipt note status (pending GRN, GRN created, closed)

#### 2.3 Floor Inventory Tracking
- ❌ View floor inventory (materials received, not yet issued)
- ❌ Track material locations on floor
- ❌ Monitor aging materials (received long ago, not issued)

#### 2.4 Coordination with GRNIncharge
- ❌ Notify GRNIncharge when materials received
- ❌ Provide receipt details to GRNIncharge
- ❌ Support GRN creation process

**API Endpoints Required**: ~20
- PO expected deliveries: 2 endpoints
- Receipt note CRUD: 8 endpoints (create, list, edit, submit, view, add item, remove item, upload photo)
- Floor inventory: 4 endpoints
- Receipt-GRN coordination: 3 endpoints
- Floor dashboard: 2 endpoints
- Receipt tracking: 1 endpoint

**Legacy Features**: 
- `/PlantIndentIsuueNote/indent_for_floorIncharge_pz.jsp` (receive materials)
- `/PlantIndentIsuueNote/plant_floor_incharge_list.jsp` (receipt notes list)
- `/PlantIndentIsuueNote/floor_manger_indent_request.jsp` (coordinate with managers)

**Current Status**: ❌ 0% - No receipt note or floor inventory module

# 3. DataEntryOperator Role

## Role Overview

**Role ID**: 9  
**Database Field**: `data_entry_operator` (session variable: `#session.DataEntryOperator`)  
**Hierarchy Position**: Data entry staff, limited to specific data entry tasks  
**Primary Responsibility**: Enter material receipts, record transactions, data entry with field-level constraints

### Key Characteristics
- **Constrained role**: Limited field edit rights
- Cannot approve or reject anything
- Cannot delete data (only create/update)
- Validation-heavy (mandatory fields enforced)
- Typically works under supervision of FloorIncharge or GRNIncharge

### User Stories Summary

#### 3.1 Material Receipt Data Entry
- ❌ Enter receipt data from physical documents
- ❌ Record material quantities (with UOM validation)
- ❌ Enter receipt dates (must be <= today)
- ❌ Enter vendor details (from dropdown, cannot add new)
- ❌ **Cannot** edit approved records

#### 3.2 Transaction Recording
- ❌ Record material movements (internal transfers)
- ❌ Enter material issue details (from issue notes)
- ❌ Record material adjustments (with supervisor approval)

#### 3.3 Data Validation & Correction
- ❌ View data entry errors
- ❌ Correct draft entries before submission
- ❌ Cannot edit after submission (supervisor must approve correction)

**API Endpoints Required**: ~12
- Receipt data entry: 4 endpoints (create, edit draft, view, submit)
- Transaction recording: 3 endpoints
- Movement recording: 2 endpoints
- Validation: 2 endpoints
- Data correction: 1 endpoint

**Legacy Features**: 
- `/PlantIndentIsuueNote/plant_Deo_list.jsp` (Data Entry Operator list)
- Constrained forms with mandatory field validation
- Read-only fields after submission

**Current Status**: ❌ 0% - No data entry module with role-based constraints

## Combined Implementation Summary

- **PlantManager**: 18 endpoints

## Security Considerations

### PlantManager CAN:
- ✅ View all indents for their plant (cross-department)
- ✅ Approve plant-specific indents
- ✅ View all plant operations (receipts, issues, inventory)
- ✅ Generate plant reports
- ❌ Cannot approve POs (Procurement only)
- ❌ Cannot view other plants

### FloorIncharge CAN:
- ✅ Record material receipts for their plant
- ✅ Create receipt notes
- ✅ View floor inventory
- ✅ Coordinate with GRNIncharge
- ❌ Cannot approve GRNs (GRNIncharge only)
- ❌ Cannot issue materials (GoodsIncharge only)

### DataEntryOperator CAN:
- ✅ Enter data in assigned forms
- ✅ Edit draft entries
- ✅ View own entries
- ❌ Cannot edit submitted entries
- ❌ Cannot delete any data
- ❌ Cannot approve anything
